## Changes
- Topic mode: make a video on any topic, or from pasted video links (#9).
- Every video lasts 62 to 90 s, with a hard 90 s cap (#9, #12).
- Karaoke captions, scene cuts, retry from the failed step and a scheduled refresh of the trends (#9).
- The on-video AI label is gone; the post text and each platform's AI flag stay (#12).

## Downloads
- macOS Apple Silicon: DMG installer.
- Windows x64: EXE or MSI installer.
- In-app updates use the signed update bundles and latest.json included in this release.
