## Changes
- French dub: turn one video into a 62 to 90 s French dub. Each line is translated to fit the time of the original line, with tu or vous and the channel's glossary, the original music and sounds are kept, old burned-in subtitles are blurred, and karaoke captions are added. You can compare it side by side with the original. A dub is sent to Postiz automatically only when its rights are owned, licensed or cc; otherwise it waits at the video gate (#26).
- One voice per speaker in a dub: each speaker gets a voice from the channel profile, or one you pick on the project page (#27).
- Tools page: download a video, transcribe it, translate subtitles, read a text aloud, or burn subtitles into a video. A finished job can feed the next one (#28).
- Stats page: ElevenLabs characters and estimated cost per project, channel and tool, with this month's cost, the last 30 days and the cost per video. Settings has the price per 1,000 characters and a monthly budget: the page warns at 80%, and at 100% only the automatic making pauses (#29).
- Windows: script and cache files that hold Chinese text are now read and written as UTF-8 (#28).
