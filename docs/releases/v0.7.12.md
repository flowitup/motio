## Changes
- AI clips: some scenes of an AI video can now be 5 s clips made by fal's H3 Max from the scene's picture, instead of a slow camera move. They are off by default. Set how many (0 to 6) per channel under Channels → AI clips per video, or for one video in New video → AI video → AI clips. The scenes are spread over the video and the first one is always included. A clip fal can't make is tried twice, then the scene keeps its picture and camera move. Each new clip costs $0.08 per second by default (Settings → AI pictures), shows in Stats and the project page, and counts toward the monthly budget. With clips the post reads "Voix off générée par IA. Images et vidéos générées par IA." (#35).
- Windows PC as the 24/7 engine: a runbook (`docs/WINDOWS_SERVER.md`, in Vietnamese) and a script (`tools/windows/motio-server.ps1`, run in an elevated PowerShell) that run the installed engine in the background through Task Scheduler. It listens on the Tailscale address only, opens the port for Tailscale addresses only, keeps the data and logs on the drive you pick, and has `-KeepAwake`, `-Status` and `-Uninstall`. The Mac app then works as a viewer through Settings → Remote engine (#39).
- Cookie file: a new Settings → Cookie file (`YTDLP_COOKIES_FILE`) takes a Netscape `cookies.txt` for an engine that has no browser, such as a server or a Windows PC. It is checked when saved, used for pasted links and for Bilibili search, downloads and followed spaces, and wins over the browser option. The media route never serves it (#39).
- Settings → Engine status now shows the free and total space of the engine's data drive (`/api/health`) and says that downloads, projects and settings stay on the engine's computer when the engine is remote (#39).
- Fix: when the voice step rewrote or trimmed an AI video's script, the render could use the pictures of the old script. The pictures are now made or reused for the final script right after the voice (#35).
- Fix: a short source clip under a longer scene is held on its last frame for the whole scene, so the video no longer ends up shorter than the voice (#35).
