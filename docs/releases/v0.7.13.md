## Changes
- Bilibili trending lists: New videos → Bilibili trending follows Bilibili's own ranking (one of 17 categories, or all), Popular, or the weekly must-watch, then Follow list. It needs no login and keeps original uploads only (reposts, "no reprint" and paid videos are left out). A newly followed list shows its top 20, then only videos Motio has not seen. The list names follow the app language (#42).
- HeyGen Video 1 as a second AI clip provider: Settings → AI pictures → Clip provider switches the AI clips from fal H3 Max to HeyGen (image to video, 768p, 5 s; needs `HEYGEN_API_KEY`; default price $0.02 per second, counted in Stats and the monthly budget). HeyGen's commercial terms are not confirmed, so a video with HeyGen clips always stops at Awaiting video approval. A paid HeyGen job is remembered, so a retry does not pay twice. `uv run python -m motio clipcheck <picture> "<scene>" [fal | heygen]` makes one trial clip (about $0.10) to compare the two (#42).
- Quality check after every render: the length is 62–90 s, the picture is 1080×1920 H.264, the sound is about -14 LUFS without clipping, no silence over 1.5 s, and the first picture is not black. A problem stops the video at Awaiting video approval when the channel sends to Postiz (you can still approve it); a warning is only shown. The project page has a Quality card, the Slack "waiting for approval" message says why, and a source, title or script close to one used in the last 30 days is flagged (#42).
- Add a video file: next to every place that takes links (New video, a project's sources, a trending topic's links), send a video you already have, from Douyin or anywhere, and use it like a link (dub, or a source for a topic explainer). MP4, MOV, M4V, MKV, WebM, AVI, TS or FLV up to 2 GB; anything but MP4 is converted. Rights stay unknown until you set them, and a file is never credited publicly (#42).
- Series and recurring characters for AI videos: Channels → Cast (one `Name: look` line per fictional character, up to 8) and Series (the premise). Every scene whose picture prompt names a character gets that exact description in front of its prompt, and each new AI video of the channel is written as the next episode, knowing the recap of the last six; the project page shows the episode number and recap. It makes the characters alike, it does not guarantee the same face (#43).
- Fix: a video file whose length the file does not state was refused as "no video to use"; it is accepted now. A half-written upload is cleaned up, including after a stopped engine (#42).
- Fix: the black-picture check now sees a black video even with the title, badge and captions drawn on it, and the repeat warning covers a full 30 days on a busy install (#42).
- Fix: the Douyin message says fresh browser cookies are needed, not a logged-in account (#42).
