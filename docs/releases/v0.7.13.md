## Changes
- Bilibili trending lists: New videos → Followed sources (open the card) → Bilibili trending follows Bilibili's own ranking (one of 16 categories, or all of them), Popular, or the weekly must-watch: pick a list, then Follow list. It needs no login and keeps original uploads only (reposts, "no reprint" and paid videos are left out). A newly followed list shows its top 20, then only videos Motio has not seen. The list names follow the app language (#42).
- HeyGen Video 1 as a second AI clip provider: Settings → AI pictures → Clip provider switches the AI clips from fal H3 Max to HeyGen (image to video, 768p, 5 s; needs `HEYGEN_API_KEY`; default price $0.02 per second, counted in Stats and the monthly budget). HeyGen's commercial terms are not confirmed, so a video with HeyGen clips is not sent to Postiz on its own: it stops at Awaiting video approval when the channel sends to Postiz. A paid HeyGen job is remembered, so a retry does not pay twice. `uv run python -m motio clipcheck <picture> "<scene>" [fal | heygen]` makes one paid trial clip (about $0.10 with HeyGen, about $0.40 with fal) so you can compare the two (#42).
- Quality check after every render. A video that is outside 62–90 s, has no picture or no sound, is almost silent, starts on a black picture (it sees it even with the title, badge and captions drawn on top) or cannot be read stops at Awaiting video approval when the channel sends to Postiz (you can still approve it). Everything else is a warning that is only shown: a picture that is not 1080×1920 H.264, loudness away from -14 LUFS, clipping, a silence over 1.5 s, a black stretch, and a source, title or script close to one used in the last 30 days. The project page has a Quality card, and the Slack "waiting for approval" message says why (#42).
- Add a video file: next to every place that takes links (New video, a project's sources, a trending topic's links), send a video you already have, from Douyin or anywhere, and use it like a link (dub, or a source for a topic explainer). MP4, MOV, M4V, MKV, WebM, AVI, TS or FLV up to 2 GB; anything but MP4 is converted, and a file that does not state its length (a screen recording) is accepted. Rights stay unknown until you set them, and a file is never credited publicly. Douyin links still need fresh browser cookies for yt-dlp (not a logged-in account); without them, add the video as a file (#42).
- Series and recurring characters for AI videos: Channels → Cast (one `Name: look` line per fictional character, up to 8). Every scene whose picture prompt names a character gets that exact description in front of its prompt. Add a Series premise and each new AI video of the channel is written as the next episode, knowing the recap of the last six; the project page then shows the episode number and recap. It makes the characters alike, it does not guarantee the same face (#43).

## Downloads
- macOS Apple Silicon: DMG installer.
- Windows x64: EXE or MSI installer.
- In-app updates use the signed update bundles and latest.json included in this release. The update replaces the engine inside the app only: a remote engine (the Hetzner server, or the Windows PC) has to be updated on its own, by a deploy or by reinstalling its MSI. Until it is, the new lists, file upload and cast do not work on it (Follow list adds a plain search instead of the list, and a channel saved with a cast loses it). From this release the engine reports its real version (Settings → Engine status shows 0.7.13); an older engine shows 0.1.0.
