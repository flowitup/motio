## Changes
- **Claude is called through the Anthropic API only** (#62). `claude -p` and the "Claude Code" provider are gone, so the engine needs an `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (Settings, or `.env` on a server) and every script, translation, score and pick is billed per token. Two model tiers, both set in Settings: `LLM_MODEL` for scripts, translations and AI scenes (default `claude-opus-5-5`) and `LLM_MODEL_FAST` for scoring trends and new videos and picking sources (default `claude-haiku-5-5`). The old short names `sonnet`, `opus` and `haiku` still work and now mean the current Claude 5.5 model; `LLM_PROVIDER` and `ANTHROPIC_MODEL` are ignored. Each call is recorded with its tokens and estimated USD, so its cost is added to the USD totals on Stats and counts in the monthly budget. A Claude call that receives nothing for 4 minutes is cut and retried (up to 4 times), and an answer that is cut short fails at once instead of being parsed half-way. Starting a video, an AI video or a dub from the app without the Claude key, or without the ElevenLabs key on Windows or Linux (a Mac can still read with the built-in voice), is refused up front with a message, instead of failing after minutes of downloading; the command line and auto-make do not make that check.
- **Review the pictures of an AI video before the voice** (#59). New video → AI video has a *Review the pictures first* switch (on by default in the app). The video stops once its pictures are made: each shot can be approved, un-approved or redone on its own (a new seed or an edited prompt; only that picture is made and paid for), and *Continue* records the voice and renders the video when every shot is approved. A picture the provider cannot make is noted and the others go on. Slack gets a "pictures ready for your review" message.
- **Same faces and a cast per video** (#59). The AI video form has a *Characters* box (one `Name: look` line per fictional character, added to the channel's cast) and *Same faces in every scene* (on by default). Each character gets one reference portrait (made with the picked fal text model) and every scene that names characters (up to 3) is made from their portraits with fal `qwen-image-edit-2511`; the review screen shows the portraits with *New portrait* and *Look* buttons. Modal scenes stay text only.
- **Pick the fal picture model in Settings** (#66): Seedream 5 Pro (default, about $0.07 a picture), Nano Banana 2 / 2.1 / Pro, GPT Image 2 and FLUX.3. Qwen-Image is removed from the list. **No fal model is marked as cleared for a monetized channel yet** (`images.CLEARED_FAL_MODELS` is empty), so an AI video made with fal pictures that would go to Postiz stops at the video gate until someone reads a model's terms and adds it to that list.
- **Explainers built on other people's videos** whose rights are not owned, licensed or CC (`unknown` counts as not owned) now stop at the video gate before they are sent to Postiz, like a dub.
- **Engine safety** (#63): `/media` serves only the `projects`, `tools` and `cache` folders of the data folder (never the database, settings or models); a render FFmpeg run is cut after 45 minutes and the audio extraction for Whisper after 20; the voice request is retried on a dropped connection, a timeout, a 429 or a 5xx; trends that got no score are left for the next refresh instead of being saved with 0; a finished video can be sent to Postiz again as its channel is set (`POST /api/projects/{id}/resend`).
- **App review pass** (#62, #64, #65): Trending opens the project that already exists for a topic, lets you hide topics, and shows setup and budget notices; Projects filters by channel and kind, searches, loads more, shows why a project failed on its card, and shows a rail badge for projects that failed in the last 7 days; a project page has an approve bar under the header, saves unsaved script edits when you approve, shows a failure banner, offers downloads, has keyboard shortcuts and full screen on the video (the keys work while the picture has focus), and asks before "post now"; the Explainer form of New video makes one video per topic line and shows a cost estimate; "New videos" is now called **Followed**, with hide-many, source rights and an in-app remove dialog; Settings is grouped into sections with an Advanced fold and warns before you leave with unsaved changes; the Channels form is in collapsible groups with a sticky action bar and *Duplicate channel*; the Tools tab strip, Remove logo and the page columns are tidied. Focus rings, labels, skip link and tab keys were fixed for keyboard use. English and Vietnamese strings are in both catalogs.
- **Readability** (#60): the smallest type is 12 px (only the small count badges on the rail use 11 px), card titles use the same strip as the Studio panels, the rail is 80 px wide, motion respects the system's reduced-motion setting. The design rules are in `docs/DESIGN.md` and `docs/UI_REVIEW.md`.
- **New app icon and rail mark** (#55): logo C (a waveform shaped like an M).
- Server and docs: `deploy/compose.yaml` and `deploy/env.example` pass `LLM_MODEL` and `LLM_MODEL_FAST` instead of `LLM_PROVIDER` and `ANTHROPIC_MODEL`; the Windows PC runbook no longer asks for Claude Code.

## Before you use it
- **Enter your Claude API key** in Settings → `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`. Without it nothing that writes a script or scores a topic works (the app and the engine now say so up front).
- **A remote engine must be on this version before the 0.7.16 app connects to it** (the Hetzner server and the Windows PC; the in-app update replaces only the engine inside the app). The new screens call routes an older engine does not have (project counts, filtered project list, hide a trend, picture review, resend). Settings → Engine status shows the version the engine reports. On the server, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` must be in `/opt/motio/.env` (it already was for the API provider); `LLM_PROVIDER` and `ANTHROPIC_MODEL` are ignored now, so move any `ANTHROPIC_MODEL` value into `LLM_MODEL`, or the server uses Opus 5.5. The Windows PC needs the new MSI and a rerun of `motio-server.ps1`, plus the key in Settings; Claude Code is no longer needed there.
- AI videos made with fal pictures are held at the video gate before Postiz, whichever model you pick (a channel without Postiz sends nothing, so there is nothing to hold). An AI project made on 0.7.15 is held the same way if you render it again before it was sent.

## Verification
- Automated tests on the release commit (Linux, Python 3.12): **646 passed** with FFmpeg on PATH, and **619 passed and 27 skipped** with FFmpeg and ffprobe hidden, as on the CI runners; `ruff check motio tests` clean; `pnpm build` succeeds and the built app contains the 15 IBM Plex font files and no link to an outside font host. GitHub CI (ruff and pytest on Ubuntu and Windows, `pnpm build`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`) runs on the release pull request.
- The frozen engine was built with PyInstaller on Linux (up to the FFmpeg download step, which Linux does not support) and a Claude call was made from inside it against a local stand-in server: the Anthropic SDK (1.8.0) streamed an answer and the usage row was written; over HTTPS it needs the machine's CA store, so a private CA is refused unless `SSL_CERT_FILE` points at it.
- Looked at before the merge in earlier pull requests: Chromium screenshots of the new screens against a real engine with the placeholder picture provider, in English and Vietnamese.
- **Known limits, left for a later release:** an AI project made on 0.7.15 keeps provider `fal` and is now shown (and priced) as Seedream, although its old pictures were Qwen-Image and are reused when it is rendered again; changing the picture provider or the fal model in Settings between "pictures ready" and *Continue* makes every picture again (and charges for it); the project page's "voice ≈ $" figure leaves Claude's cost out only when the engine reports it separately (an older remote engine includes it); one failed Claude batch (rate limit, out of tokens) still stops that whole trend refresh; `.env.example` still lists `LLM_PROVIDER=claude_cli` (harmless, Claude sessions cannot edit that file) and `docs/APP_PLAN.md` still describes Qwen-Image as the cleared fal model.
- **Not tried, and the reasons to look at this release first on the Mac:**
  - a real Claude API call (no key in the cloud), including the default models `claude-opus-5-5` and `claude-haiku-5-5` and the effort setting sent with them;
  - real fal pictures: the new models in the picker (their request fields were taken from fal's pages and their prices taken or worked out from them; nothing was run), `qwen-image-edit-2511` with reference portraits (how well it keeps a face, its price, whether it takes data URIs as documented), and real picture generation through the review screen;
  - the app in a real Tauri window on macOS and on Windows (video playback in the custom player, fonts in the packaged app, narrow layout, the new keyboard shortcuts and full screen);
  - the frozen engine on macOS and Windows (only Linux was built; the HTTPS trust store there is the operating system's);
  - the Windows PC and Hetzner engines on this version, and `cargo clippy` locally (CI runs it);
  - the in-app update from 0.7.15 to 0.7.16.
