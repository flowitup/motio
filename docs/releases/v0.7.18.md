## Changes
- **Trending lists videos by category or tag** (#72). The Trending screen now shows videos that are hot on Bilibili in the categories you follow (Tech, AI & tools, AI films & series, Series & films, Knowledge & science, Lifestyle & savoir-vivre, Food, Travel) or under any Chinese search word, most played of the last 7 days. A category is a ranking and / or a few Chinese tags; the ranking part of a category skips videos older than 30 days. Each video has *Dub in French* (voice separated, music kept) and *Create* (an explainer). They are ordinary followed sources, so scoring, New videos and the rights gates work as before: nothing from here is sent automatically, a person approves first.
- **The source's Chinese voice is removed** from an explainer made from a followed video: it is muted by default (a switch in the card's make panel turns it back on). A dub already separated the voice and kept the music.
- **China's hot news (NewsNow) is off by default** (`NEWS_TOPICS`, Settings). When it is on, a *News topics* page and the refresh of it come back.
- **Bilibili's "no reprint" tick is ignored.** Bilibili ticks it on about 99 % of videos, so it says nothing; it neither hides a video nor adds a flag, and the rights of a video stay Unknown. Followed Bilibili ranking lists used to drop those videos and now show them.
- **A followed account has its own page, by day.** On Followed, a click on an account (a YouTube channel or playlist, a Bilibili space, a search) opens every video Motio has seen from it, newest upload first, under one heading per day (Today, Yesterday, the date). *Check now* on that page checks only that account. YouTube upload times are read from the channel's or playlist's RSS feed (its latest 15 videos), Bilibili's from the video page; a video whose time could not be read is last, under "Date unknown". Douyin and Facebook accounts still cannot be followed.

## Before you use it
- **A remote engine has to be updated** (the Hetzner server and the Windows PC): the in-app update replaces only the engine inside the app, and the new Trending screen and the account page call engine routes (`/api/categories`, `/api/watches/{id}/videos`) that an older engine does not have.
- **`.env.example` is not updated**: add `NEWS_TOPICS=` by hand if you keep a copy of it.
- Followed Bilibili ranking lists now include videos that carry the "no reprint" tick; whether you may reuse a video stays your call.

## Verification
- Automated tests on the release commit: **661 passed** (FFmpeg on PATH); `ruff check motio tests` clean; `pnpm build` succeeds. CI runs the same on Ubuntu and Windows plus `cargo clippy`.
- Live, against real Bilibili from a sandbox and on the Mac: category and tag fetches return real videos; an explainer made from a trending clip came out with French-only audio (checked with Whisper), about $1 of API cost. The Trending screen was used in a browser against the engine in English and Vietnamese.
- The account page was only tried in a browser with seeded data (Today, Yesterday and older days, hidden and unknown-date videos); the YouTube RSS read and the Bilibili date are covered by tests with stand-ins, not by a real account.
- **Not tried:** the in-app update from 0.7.17, the Windows build and the Hetzner server on this version, *Dub in French* from a trending video, and the app in a real Tauri window.
- **Known limits, left for a later release:** Trending shows "Checking…" with empty lists until every category has answered (about 80 s the first time); an explainer from a short source clip may reuse clips to reach 62–90 s; many listed videos are longer than 10 minutes; a download that stopped half way can leave a stale `.part` file and show the unhelpful "Could not download any source video: ERROR:"; an account page starts with the latest 15 videos per list and grows with each check; the 0.7.17 leftovers (Anthropic workspace ID setting, ElevenLabs voice list message, "Monthly voice budget" label).
