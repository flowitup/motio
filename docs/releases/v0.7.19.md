## Changes
- **More Chinese sites for pasted links** (#75, #76). Paste a link from Xiaohongshu, Weibo, Xigua, Kuaishou, Haokan or Zhihu anywhere a link goes (New video, a project's sources, the Tools page). Link shapes yt-dlp does not understand are fixed first (Weibo `weibo.com/2/detail/<id>`, Xigua ids), a `xhslink.com` short link is followed once, and Haokan videos are read from the address in the phone page. A Xigua link is tried on Douyin first under the same id (no cookies needed when the video is also there). Kuaishou goes through the separate **videodl** program when you have installed it (`pipx install videofetch`, or `MOTIO_VIDEODL`); Motio only runs its Kuaishou client and never bundles it (PolyForm Noncommercial licence). When a site wants a login, the error points to the cookie settings. There is no followed list or feed for these sites. Details and what was tried: `docs/CN_PLATFORMS.md`.
- **Original sound level** (#74). A project made from source footage has an *Original sound* card with a 0–100 % slider: how loud the source's own sound is under the French voice (Motio's level is 10 %; a video made from a trending clip has none unless you choose). *Save and re-render* renders again with the voice the video already has, so nothing is recorded or paid again. Dubs and AI videos do not have the card.
- **French dub of the whole video** (#77). The dub form (New video, the video card in Followed / Trending, and the project page) has a *Length* choice: *A part* as before, or *Whole video*, which dubs the video from start to end whatever its length, with no excerpt, no intro or outro and no 62–90 s window (CLI: `dub <link> whole`). Claude translates 40 lines at a time, the voice is read in batches, and the voice / music separation runs window by window. A whole dub is slower and costs more, and one longer than 90 s is never sent to Postiz on its own (Facebook Reels limit): it waits at the video gate and you send it yourself.
- **Hide text or watermark** (#80, #81). On a project's page, for each source video, you can draw boxes over burned-in text or a watermark that stays in one place: a box is blurred, optionally with one of your logos drawn over it, and a *keep area* zooms the picture so the rest leaves the frame (up to 6 boxes per source, the keep area at least a quarter of the width and height). Your logos live in *Settings → Logos* (PNG, up to 20). This is applied at render to the source picture only, under the title and French captions. Nothing is found or placed automatically. #81 fixes the *Old subtitles* box of a dub so it lines up with a source that is letterboxed in the player.
- **Mirror and colour** (#82). Per source, a switch to flip the picture left to right and a colour filter (warm, cool, black and white, sepia, vivid, faded), with a preview; one setting covers a whole AI video, and *Same for every source* copies it. *Save and re-render* applies it. Like the boxes above, it is turned on by hand and never automatic.
- **No source-rights declaration** (#83). The app no longer asks for the rights of a source video: the field is gone from New video (explainer and dub), the project's source card and Followed, and no rights gate holds a video any more. A video is held at its gate only for the reasons that remain: the channel's own script / video gate, an AI picture provider that is not cleared for monetized channels, a failed quality check, or a whole-video dub longer than 90 s. Old saved values are ignored.

## Before you use it
- **A remote engine has to be updated** (the Hetzner server and the Windows PC): the in-app update replaces only the engine inside the app, and the new cards call engine routes that an older engine does not have.
- Nothing needs to be set in `.env`. Kuaishou links only work if `videodl` is installed on the machine that runs the engine.
