## Changes
- **Douyin home feed on the Trending screen** (#87). Trending has a new list, *Douyin · Home feed*, that you follow like a Bilibili category. It reads the home recommendation feed that Douyin shows a visitor with no login: f2 signs the request (the same guest session a Douyin download uses), five answers of about ten videos are read per check, and photo posts, ads and videos under 15 s are left out. It is a fresh draw of what Douyin recommends, **not a ranking**, and has no categories; the screen says so. *Make video* goes through the Douyin download that already existed (f2 first, yt-dlp second). When Douyin answers nothing, or the signature is out of date, the error shows on the source.
- **Weibo hot videos on the Trending screen** (#87). A second new list, *Weibo · Hot videos*, reads Weibo's hot timeline. Weibo answers it to a visitor once the browser has asked Weibo's own guest endpoint for a session (one request, nothing signed). About half of the posts are videos; only those of at least 15 s with a caption are kept. It is a fresh draw, not a ranking, with no categories. *Make video* downloads the file straight from the mp4 address the list gave (an http address on Weibo's own CDN, kept for 2 days in `data/cache/weibo_media.json`), so yt-dlp and a Weibo login are not needed for these. A Weibo link you paste yourself still goes through yt-dlp when the list never gave that post.
- **Fetch videos from** (#85 row, extended in #87). The source switches at the top of Trending now also list Douyin and Weibo. Switching one on when nothing of it is followed yet follows its list (Douyin: Home feed; Weibo: Hot videos).
- Xiaohongshu, Xigua, Haokan and Zhihu still have no list a plain visitor can read, so they stay paste-a-link. What was tried is in `docs/CN_PLATFORMS.md`.

## Before you use it
- **A remote engine has to be updated** (the Hetzner server and the Windows PC): the in-app update replaces only the engine inside the app, and the two new lists call engine code that an older engine does not have. Settings → Engine status shows the engine's version.
- Nothing needs to be set in `.env`. The Douyin list needs the same `f2` package as Douyin downloads (it is part of the installer and of `uv sync`).

## Verification
- Automated tests on the release commit: **791 passed** with FFmpeg on PATH; `ruff check motio tests` clean; `pnpm build` succeeds. CI runs the tests without FFmpeg on Ubuntu and Windows, plus `cargo clippy`.
- The Douyin list was read live on the owner's Mac (f2 signed request). The Weibo list was read and one video downloaded and probed (852x480 h264 + aac) from a cloud sandbox, and its guest session and timeline were checked on the owner's Mac in Chrome and in Python.
- **Not tried:** the Trending screen, the source switches and the two new lists in the installed app, the Windows build, Weibo's list from the installed engine, and everything listed as not tried in v0.7.20 that has not been checked since (the Kuaishou list from the owner's own network, whole-video dub with real ElevenLabs and Claude, logged-in cookies, the in-app update from 0.7.20).
- **Known limits, left for a later release:** the three lists are random draws, so a video seen once may not come back; a Douyin answer can fail when f2's signature goes out of date (bump f2, see `docs/DOUYIN_F2.md`); Trending still shows "Checking…" until every category has answered; a download that stopped half way can leave a stale `.part` file; the 0.7.17 leftovers (Anthropic workspace ID setting, ElevenLabs voice list message).
- **Not in this release:** the AI video length picker (#79) and the removal of the daily video cap, source credits and `NEWS_SOURCES` (#78) are still open pull requests.
