## Changes
- **Google Drive as the main storage** (#92). Settings has a new *Storage (Google Drive)* section. With "Use Google Drive as main storage" on, a finished project's big files (every file of 1 MiB or more: the 9:16 and 16:9 videos, voice, AI pictures and clips) and the source videos that no project in progress needs are uploaded to your own Google Drive and then removed from the machine. Each upload is checked against the size and md5 that Drive reports before anything is deleted; "Keep a copy on this machine" turns Drive into a backup only. The app plays moved videos straight from Drive, and a re-render, retry or script edit brings back what it needs first. Deleting a project puts its Drive files in the Drive bin. Motio uses the `drive.file` permission, so it only sees the files it made. Setup (once): `docs/GOOGLE_DRIVE.md`.
- **Make video opens the New video form** (#89). *Make video* on a Trending or Followed card now opens the New video page for that clip, with the same Explainer and Dub forms as a new project: an editable topic (empty = made from this clip alone), "Use only this video", "Remove the original voice", the length picker, and for a dub the whole video or a part from / to.
- **AcFun and Pear Video are back** (#90) as Trending sources, each with its switch in "Fetch videos from", and as pasted links. AcFun is its daily ranking (all sections or one of eight); Pear Video is its home page list, whose rows can be only a few seconds long.
- **Settings are simpler** (#78). "News sources", "Max videos per day" and the two source credit switches are gone, with the code behind them. Videos behave as with the old defaults: every news source, no daily cap, no source on the video or in the post. `sources.txt` is still written for every project, and a channel's own auto-make daily cap stays.

## Before you use it
- **Google Drive needs a one-time setup**: a Google Cloud OAuth client of type "TVs and Limited Input devices", the app published "In production", then the Client ID and secret pasted in Settings → Storage and *Sign in with Google*. Steps in `docs/GOOGLE_DRIVE.md`. Drive is off until you turn it on.
- **A remote engine has to be updated** (the Hetzner server and the Windows PC): the in-app update replaces only the engine inside the app. Each engine signs in to Drive on its own, from the app connected to it.
