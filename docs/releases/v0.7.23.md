## Changes
- **A new look** (#95). The app keeps its layout and features but is no longer one amber-on-dark workbench: every section has its own colour, hot pink is the action colour, and the left rail has tinted icon tiles with a pink badge for videos waiting for you and a red-orange badge with a warning sign for recent failures. Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono replace IBM Plex (bundled, works offline). Settings, Channels, Stats, Tools and Remove logo were not looked at in the new colours.
- **Scan screen** (#91). A new *Scan* page reads one platform's accounts, hashtags or keywords (one to ten lines) into a table with cover, video ID, title, account, likes, views, length and date. You can search the table, set a minimum number of views, sort, select rows or a range, *Download N selected* (one Tools download job each), *Follow these accounts*, and export CSV. Nothing is saved or made by the scan itself. YouTube and Bilibili go through yt-dlp and Bilibili's tag search.
- **Follow Kuaishou, Douyin and Weibo accounts** (#91). *Followed* accepts an account link (or `<site>:user:<id>`) from those three sites, like a YouTube channel. Weibo uses its guest session, Douyin goes through f2, and Kuaishou's account page answers only a signed-in browser, so it uses your own cookies (cookie file or browser, Settings); a slider captcha is reported and never solved.
- **Keyword and hashtag scan on Chinese platforms** (#91). Weibo uses its own search with the guest session (no login). Douyin uses its own search signed by f2 when your douyin.com cookies are set up, and otherwise reads the public search site `so.douyin.com` in a headless Chrome with no account. Kuaishou's search needs your own cookies. The Douyin no-account reader needs Google Chrome, Chromium or Edge installed on the machine that runs the engine (the engine does not bundle a browser; `MOTIO_CHROME` can name one).
- **Pick the length of an AI video** (#79). New video > AI video > Length has *Your length*: any length from 30 s to 30 min next to the 70 / 80 / 90 s presets, which keep the 62–90 s rule. Up to 2 min 30 s the script is fitted to ±10 % of the length; a longer video is written part by part (a plan, then about 75 s per part) and is never fitted or trimmed afterwards. The render does not pad it to 62 s. A long video makes one picture per line, so it costs much more than an 80 s one. Any finished video longer than 90 s waits at the video gate and is never sent to Postiz on its own (Facebook Reels limit). CLI: `ai "topic" 300`.

### Also in this release (v0.7.22 was tagged but never published, so these come with it)
- **Google Drive as the main storage** (#92). Settings > *Storage (Google Drive)*: with it on, a finished project's big files (1 MiB or more) and source videos no project in progress needs are uploaded to your own Drive after a size and md5 check, then removed from the machine ("Keep a copy on this machine" turns Drive into a backup). The app plays moved videos from Drive and a re-render or retry brings back what it needs. Motio uses the `drive.file` permission only. Setup (once): `docs/GOOGLE_DRIVE.md`.
- **Make video opens the New video form** (#89) for that clip, with the same Explainer and Dub forms as a new project.
- **AcFun and Pear Video are back** (#90) as Trending sources with their own switches, and as pasted links.
- **Settings are simpler** (#78). "News sources", "Max videos per day" and the two source credit switches are gone with the code behind them; `sources.txt` is still written for every project.

## Before you use it
- **A remote engine has to be updated** (the Hetzner server and the Windows PC): the in-app update replaces only the engine inside the app, and the Scan screen, account follows and the AI length call engine routes an older engine does not have. Settings > Engine status shows the engine's version. Each engine signs in to Google Drive on its own.
- **Google Drive needs a one-time setup** (OAuth client of type "TVs and Limited Input devices", app published "In production", Client ID and secret in Settings > Storage); Drive is off until you turn it on.
