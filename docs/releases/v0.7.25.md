## Changes
- **New: Clip mode** (#102). On *New video* there is a new *Clips* tab. Give it the link of a long video (a stream recording, a trailer, a reaction video) and Motio cuts the best moments into vertical 9:16 clips that **keep the original sound**: no voice-over, no ElevenLabs. Two ways to use it:
  - **Claude picks for me**: Motio downloads the video, transcribes it, Claude chooses the best moments (you set how many, 1 to 12), and one project per clip appears in Projects, through your channel's gates and Postiz like any video.
  - **I'll pick myself**: the *Clip Studio* screen opens with Claude's suggestions on a timeline. Drag the start and end marks (they snap to scene cuts), add or remove moments, move the 9:16 frame over the picture (one position, or several so the frame glides), edit the hook, choose the captions, then *Make N clips*.
- **Each clip lasts 15 s to 1 min 30 s.** This is the only mode that can be shorter than 1 min 2 s; every other video keeps its 62–90 s rule.
- **The 9:16 frame follows the face.** The first positions come from a face detector (YuNet through OpenCV, a 230 KB model downloaded once, free, runs on your machine). No face found, or a source that is already vertical: the frame stays centred. *Follow the face* in Clip Studio and on the project page finds the positions again for the current part, and every position can still be changed by hand. Or switch the frame off and show the whole picture over a blurred background.
- Captions come from what is said in the video (translated to French when it is not French; or as spoken; or none), the hook is the title band on top, and the sound is only levelled. The post text has no "Voix off générée par IA." because there is no AI voice. The quality check runs on clips as well. A clip is sent on its own only through a channel with Postiz and its video gate off. In a terminal: `python -m motio clip <link> [count]`.
- A project made this way has a *Clip* card on its page (start, end, hook, captions, frame; *Save and render again*).

## Before you use it
- **How to use it:** *New video* > *Clips* > paste the link > *Claude picks for me* (clips appear in Projects) or *I'll pick myself* (Clip Studio). It needs the Anthropic key (Settings), like the other modes. A first run downloads the video and transcribes it, which takes a while for a long video; the video stays in the cache for the next clips.
- **A remote engine has to be updated** (the Hetzner server and the Windows PC): the in-app update replaces only the engine inside the app, and Clip mode lives in the engine. Settings > Engine status shows the engine's version.
- The face model is downloaded the first time a clip is analysed, so that machine needs internet access then. If it cannot be downloaded, a simpler detector shipped with OpenCV is used (it misses small faces), and it tries again after 10 minutes.
- Everything in v0.7.23 and v0.7.24 still applies.

## Verification
- Automated tests on the release commit: **938 passed** with FFmpeg on PATH; `ruff check motio tests` clean; `pnpm build` succeeds. The pull request's CI was green: tests on Ubuntu and Windows, the desktop app build, and the macOS and Windows installer builds (these freeze the engine with OpenCV).
- Clip Studio and a full export were checked in a browser against a test engine with a real FFmpeg render (1080×1920 output, original sound kept, moving frame, hook and French captions). Face tracking was checked on a real face photo moving across a test video: YuNet found faces at every size tried, including small ones the simpler detector missed.
- **Not tried:** Clip mode with real Claude and real Whisper on a real long video, face tracking on real talking-head footage (several faces, scene cuts), the installed app (the Clip screens, the frozen engine running the face detector), Vietnamese text on the new screens (strings are in both catalogs and `pnpm build` passes), and the in-app update from 0.7.24. Everything listed as not tried in v0.7.24 that has not been checked since still applies.
