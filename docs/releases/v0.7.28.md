## Changes
- **Optional AI note in the post text** (#112). A channel's form has a new *Post text* switch, *Add the AI note to the post* (on by default). Turned off, the post text leaves out the line "Voix off générée par IA." (and "Images générées par IA." on AI videos). The platforms' own AI flags (for example TikTok's) stay on either way, as before. It also applies when a script is edited and the post is written again.
- **Test the Postiz connection** (#112). Settings > Posting (Postiz) has a *Test the connection* button. It asks Postiz for its channels and says how many it found, or shows the error, so a wrong URL or key shows up before a video is sent. Save the settings first.
- **Everything from v0.7.27** (it was built but never published, so it is part of this release):
  - **New look for the whole app** (#109).
    - **Menu:** a slim icon bar on the left, like a video editor. The **+** at the top makes a new video, each section keeps its colour, and the name shows when you point at an icon. The review and failed counts sit on Projects, and settings and the engine light are at the bottom. Pages now use the full height of the window.
    - **Projects:** a **Board** (the default) with one column per stage: waiting, making, script check, video check, failed (with *Retry*) and done. A posting week along the bottom shows what Motio sent to Postiz and when. **Grid** keeps the previous view, and the choice is remembered. The *Review queue (n)* button stays at the top.
    - **Project page:** laid out like an editing desk.
      - The top line has the title, the steps (Find, Download, Transcribe, Script, Voice, Render), delete and the approve buttons.
      - On the left are the tabs *Script*, *Sources*, *Post* and *Check*. *Check* holds the quality check, *Run again* and the log. A clip shows its cut and hook in *Script*.
      - The player is in the middle, and the video's settings and its cost are on the right.
      - A timeline runs across the bottom: a ruler, a **Pictures** track with real frames from the video, Voice, the original sound level, one caption block per line and a pink playhead. Click anywhere on it to jump there.
    - **Looks:** Settings > Appearance has five new looks next to Neon: Daylight, Paper, Terminal, Aurora and Pop.
    - **Font:** the app now uses **Be Vietnam Pro**, with IBM Plex Mono for numbers, in every look. Settings > Appearance > Font can switch back to the look's own fonts, or to Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, Lexend or Archivo.
  - **New Trending sources** (#110).
    - **Douyin hot board** (热榜): the topics on the board and the recent videos of the hottest ones, with no login. It is read in Chrome on the engine's machine, like the Douyin keyword search on the Scan screen.
    - **Douyin Featured** (精选): the Featured tab's own list, about 20 videos per check, mostly long ones.
    - **Bilibili** popular, weekly must-watch and the all-category ranking can now be picked on the Trending screen.
  - Releases: only the 3 newest published releases are kept on GitHub (#108). Publishing a release removes the older ones and any stale draft. Git tags and these notes stay.

## Before you use it
- **The project page and the Projects board look different.** Nothing about how a video is made, approved or sent has changed.
- **Douyin hot board** needs Google Chrome (or Chromium / Edge) on the machine that runs the engine. Without it, that source reports the problem and the other sources still work.
- **A remote engine has to be updated** (the Hetzner server and the Windows PC). The in-app update replaces only the engine inside the app. The timeline's pictures and the new Trending sources come from the engine, so an older engine shows the timeline without pictures and does not offer the new sources. Settings > Engine status shows the engine's version.
- **The AI note switch** only changes the post text. If you turn it off, the post no longer says the voice or pictures are AI-made; the platform flags still do.
- If the v0.7.25 or v0.7.26 notes were skipped, everything in them applies to this version too.
