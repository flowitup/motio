## Changes
- **The menu is shorter** (#114, #116). Trending, News topics, Followed and Scan are tabs of one **Discover** entry; Tools and Remove logo are tabs of one **Tools** entry; Channels and Stats are tabs of Settings. The old addresses still work and open the right tab.
- **One Feed for Trending and Followed** (#116). Trending's platform switches, categories and tags are now a card at the top of the Followed page, which is called **Feed**. The rankings by section are in the same choice as the categories.
- **Settings in six tabs** (#115): General, AI services, Publishing, Storage, App & engine and Advanced (plus Channels and Stats). Each key shows whether it is connected, and a tab with unsaved changes is marked. Nothing about what a setting does has changed.
- **Gemini as a second voice** (#100). Settings > AI services has a *Voice provider* choice (Automatic, ElevenLabs or Gemini), a Gemini API key (Google AI Studio), a Gemini voice (30 voices, default Charon), the model and the price per minute used for cost estimates. Gemini reads each line in its own request, so a video makes one request per line, and the captions split a line's time by characters (no word timing). A channel's saved voice belongs to one provider: with the other one, the voice in Settings is used. With the provider left on Automatic, ElevenLabs is used when its key is set, else Gemini. ElevenLabs stays the default.
- **Bilibili downloads work again** (#99). When yt-dlp cannot read a Bilibili page, Motio downloads the video through Bilibili's own public API: one mp4 with sound, 480p, or 720p when the video has it. Series, films and live rooms still go to yt-dlp.
- **Bilibili rankings show today's videos** (#101). Bilibili redrew its categories in 2025; the old ranking ids still answered, but with a list frozen then, so Tech, Series & films, Knowledge & science and Food showed 0 videos and a followed ranking listed two-year-old videos as new. Motio now asks with today's ids. A ranking whose newest video is over 90 days old is reported as no longer updated: Life and Chinese animation have none, so they leave the list of rankings to follow.
- **Security updates** (#101). Pillow 12 to 13 and updated filelock, certifi and brotli in the lock file (`pip-audit` found 60 advisories before, none after). `shadcn` moved to the build-time dependencies, which clears the 30 advisories `pnpm audit --prod` listed.
- The README is shorter; feature and release detail moved to `docs/FEATURES.md` and `docs/RELEASING.md` (#114). The expired HeyGen $0.01 price note is gone from Settings.

## Before you use it
- **The menu and Settings look different**, as described above. Nothing about how a video is made, approved or sent has changed.
- **Gemini voice** is off until you add a Gemini key and pick it (or leave ElevenLabs set). Its costs are estimates from the audio length.
- **A remote engine has to be updated** (the Hetzner server and the Windows PC). The in-app update replaces only the engine inside the app. The Gemini voice, the Bilibili download and the Bilibili rankings live in the engine, so an older engine does not have them. Settings > App & engine shows the engine's version.
- If the v0.7.27 or v0.7.28 notes were skipped, everything in them applies to this version too.
