## Changes
- **One floating dock for the menu** (#118). The menu is a dock at the bottom of the window (icon over name) in every look. The Save bar in Settings and Channels floats above it.
- **Five new looks** (#118): Frost, Swiss, Cinema, Sunset and Clay, picked in Settings > General. Each has its own colours, shapes and dock. Neon stays the default.
- **New layouts for Feed and Projects** (#118). Feed has *Grid* and *Focus* (one big clip at a time: Enter makes the video, H hides it, left and right arrows browse). Projects have *Table* and *Inbox* (grouped by what needs you, with a detail card). The choice is remembered on this computer.
- **New app icon and logo** (#118): a paper plane taking off, in the Dock, the installers and the window.

## Before you use it
- **Only the app changed.** Nothing about how a video is made, approved or sent has changed, and the engine is the same as in v0.7.29; a remote engine (the Hetzner server, the Windows PC) does not need an update for this release.
- The project page and the New video page keep their old layout in every look.

## Verification
- `pnpm build` succeeds and CI (Ubuntu and Windows engine tests, desktop app build) is green on the pull request. The looks and the new layouts were checked in a browser with demo data.
- **Not tried:** the new looks and layouts in the installed app (the Tauri window on macOS and Windows), in Vietnamese, in a very narrow window, and the in-app update from 0.7.29.

## Downloads
- macOS Apple Silicon: DMG installer.
- Windows x64: EXE or MSI installer.
- In-app updates use the signed update bundles and latest.json included in this release.
