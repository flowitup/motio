## Changes
- **Video templates** (#120). A template keeps how a video looks, apart from what it says: the caption font, size, colours, outline, box, the word being spoken (colour, highlight box, grow, glow), where the captions sit (under the picture, upper, middle or lower third), the title band, the blurred or flat background and the camera moves of an AI video. It can also keep a voice, a length and the original-sound level.
- **13 starter templates** built in, from ideas in public caption styles (pycaps and Captioneer, both MIT, and caption guides). One of them, *Series card*, follows the look of a public Vietnamese TikTok series (heavy white capitals in the upper third, a dark green title card). Only ideas were taken, no code or files.
- **Make your own**: Tools > Templates has an editor with a live preview, duplicate, delete, and copy / import as JSON. *Save as template* on a finished video keeps its look.
- **Pick one**: on New video (all four kinds), in Feed and Clip Studio, as a channel's *Default template*, or on the project page (another template renders the video again with the voice it already has, nothing is recorded or paid again).
- Eight bundled open-licence fonts (SIL OFL) for the captions; their licences are in `docs/TEMPLATES.md`.

- **Includes v0.7.30, which was never published** (#118): one floating dock for the menu, five new looks (Frost, Swiss, Cinema, Sunset, Clay), Grid / Focus layouts for Feed and Table / Inbox for Projects, and the paper-plane app icon. Neon stays the default look.

## Before you use it
- **The engine changed**: a remote engine (the Hetzner server, the Windows PC) needs updating to this version to use templates. Not tried: the new app against an older engine.
- A video made with no template looks exactly as before (a test compares the rendered file byte for byte with the old output). A video keeps the look it was made with: changing or deleting a template does not change videos already made.
- Not in a template: background music and transitions (Motio has neither). A template's length is saved but not filled into the form for you.

## Verification
- 1,045 engine tests, ruff, `pnpm build` and CI (Ubuntu and Windows engine tests, desktop app, macOS and Windows builds) are green on the pull request. The screens were driven in a browser in English and Vietnamese.
- **Not tried:** a full video made with a template in the installed app (Tauri window on macOS and Windows), the packaged engine finding the bundled fonts, Windows, and the in-app update from 0.7.30.
