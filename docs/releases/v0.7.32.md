## Changes
- **Source videos download at 1080 by default** (#124). Motio used to fetch most source videos at 720, which the finished 1080×1920 video then had to enlarge. The default is now 1080 on the short side (1920×1080 for a landscape video, 1080×1920 for a vertical one). Settings > General has a new *Source video quality* choice: 720, 1080 or 1440. The size limit now applies to both width and height, so a vertical video is no longer cut down to 405×720. It also applies to Douyin and Haokan downloads and to Tools > Download video, which gains a 1440p choice and starts on 1080p. It applies to the next download; videos already downloaded are not fetched again.
- **Settings is easier to use on a wide window** (#123). The Save bar sits under the content column instead of at the left edge of the window, and only shows while something is waiting to be saved. The tabs become a vertical list on the left, cards fill two columns on a wide window, and the fields inside a card pick one or two columns from the card's own width.

## Before you use it
- **Bigger files**: 1080 source videos weigh more than 720 ones, so downloads take longer and use more disk (or Google Drive) space. Pick 720 in Settings > General > Source video quality to go back to the old size. 1440 is bigger again, and many sites do not have it (the best size they have is used).
- A remote engine (the Hetzner server, the Windows PC) has to be updated for the new download default; the in-app update replaces only the engine inside the app. Settings > App & engine shows the engine's version.
- Nothing about templates, voices or how a video is made, approved or sent has changed since v0.7.31.
